## Delegated work and remaining unknowns

The case, characters, eligible stops, route shortlist, chapters, puzzle designs,
answers, final-answer format and detailed visual treatment are **delegated design
choices**, not questions for the players. Research precedes narrative commitment.

**Research tasks:** starting permissions; concealed route access/parking; short
walks and driving; seasonal hours, daylight and changes; source-backed reference
material and reuse rights. These must be resolved or explicitly block readiness.

**Open practical information:** exact play date/weekday, iPhone/iOS version,
whether an independent app tester becomes available, and any firm delivery date.
None is needed to repeat discovery or begin research. No physical helper,
scheduled recheck, clear-weather forecast, monitoring service or precise release
date has been promised. Work may proceed intermittently until January 2027.
