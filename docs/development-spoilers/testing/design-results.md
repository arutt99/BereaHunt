## Author's desk solution review

This was a design review of the supplied inputs and reasoning, **not** an
independent human playtest. Each puzzle was traced from the intended player
inputs to its answer and alternative eliminations in `story-and-puzzles.md`.

- Stable job/serial IDs, not decorative paper damage, disambiguate documents.
- All cipher knowledge/keying conventions are supplied; no guessed extraction
  order, date-as-password or required search remains.
- Real facts are narrow documentary references. The bench experiments and
  inventory aliases are explicitly fictional; the two-quarry diagram is not
  presented as a real survey or current underwater observation.
- P05 does not infer an object's manufacture date from rock age or a relocated
  civic object. The serial-linked signed modern commission supplies that fact.
- The final order/actor deduction has every indispensable input before submit;
  no later confession, hidden conspirator or unannounced security assumption.
- All-reveal and all-reference modeled paths retain the explanation and finale.
  The separate reference action leaves selection/decoding/deduction unsolved.
- Relevant earlier evidence is listed for automatic surfacing, so the ending
  does not rely on remembering a tiny detail. Final submission remains two fields.
- Phone working aids and non-drag alternatives cover the three manipulations;
  no pen, printout, audio, second phone, staff role or prop collection.

## Remaining coverage, not claimed

image/art legibility check, browser execution, iPhone Safari/standalone test,
service-worker/offline relaunch test, physical walkthrough or deployment has
occurred in phase 4. The validator exercises the design model; it does not
execute an engine, actual persistence, browser answer parsing or cache updates.

specialist references, word lengths and the offline resource inventory. Phase 6
implements the typed answers/state/working aids. Phase 7 tests the resulting
app and assisted/unassisted experience; phase 9 addresses real-world readiness.

independent testing remains unarranged.
